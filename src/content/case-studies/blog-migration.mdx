## Problem

Vimeo's blog hosted thousands of posts dating back to the early 2000s and ran on a legacy WordPress instance maintained by an outside contractor. It was completely separate from the rest of the marketing codebase - expensive to operate, difficult to maintain, and visually out of step with a company-wide rebranding effort underway at the time.

## Approach

I took on the migration end to end. The first step was a thorough audit of every custom block type in WordPress, mapping each one to an equivalent React component in the new headless CMS. The goal wasn't just a technical lift-and-shift, the new components also needed to align with Vimeo's updated design language and use the same component library as the rest of the marketing site.

Once the component mapping was complete, I wrote a data migration script to convert the full archive of posts to the new format. With thousands of posts and varying block configurations, content integrity was a primary concern throughout.

I also coordinated across multiple stakeholders: design, marketing, and the content team, keeping everyone aligned on scope and decisions as the project progressed.

## Outcome

The migration successfully moved Vimeo's entire blog archive to the headless CMS, consolidating two separate content management systems into one. Content editors gained a significantly improved authoring experience, the blog now shares the same component library as the rest of the marketing site, and annual operating costs were reduced.
